# Jurassic Park 1993 Visual References

The park follows the first film's 1993 setting. The surrounding city is a fictional tropical gateway city added for this map, not a claimed reconstruction of Isla Nublar.

## Reference Sources

- [Art Departmental: Jurassic Park production design](https://artdepartmental.com/blog/jurassic-park-production-design). Film stills and exterior set photographs: low raw-concrete wings, a large central thatched cone and two smaller cones, dark window openings, broad entrance steps, planted concrete terraces. Local inspection image: `1993-visitor-center.jpg`.
- [Park Gate film reference](https://jurassicpark.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Gate). Primitive timber gates, heavy side pylons, fire torches and the red/yellow park name. Local inspection image `1993-main-gate.jpg` is the [film still returned by image search](https://i.redd.it/i1imotnsuuw71.jpg).
- [Agora Models: licensed Ford Explorer](https://www.agoramodels.com/jurassic-park-explorer). Movie-matched red, yellow and green paint, bubble roof, brush guard, auxiliary lights and numbered tour vehicles.

## Construction Targets

- Visitor centre: a three-cone silhouette, low concrete wings, recessed windows, planted stair terraces, fossil hall with accessible interior.
- Gate: readable block lettering with a yellow border and red face, deep timber doors, layered pylons and several torches, dense planting at its sides.
- Operations: physical CRT-style consoles, cables and cabinets, utilitarian concrete, yellow/black safety markings; concentrated lighting rather than luminous futuristic walls.
- Vehicles: recognizable green/yellow/red tour cars and grey/red utility Jeeps parked at the visitor centre and service areas.
- City: shaded arcades, balconies, shutters, tiled roofs, small shop fronts and period street furniture. Different building masses, occupied plots and connected streets replace isolated repeated boxes.
- Park ecology: palms, broad tree crowns, ferns, reeds, wet edges and rocks. Keep visible openings from the train to the dinosaurs.

These are visual targets. Static counts alone do not constitute visual acceptance; inspect the generated world at ground level and from the train.
